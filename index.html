Five steps from certificate to confirmed.

Every submission moves through the same auditable pipeline, whether it's a graduate building a profile or an employer checking a candidate.

01

Create a profile

Graduates register their identity once; employers can skip straight to verifying.

02

Submit or scan

Enter a certificate ID, upload the document, or scan its printed QR code.

03

Registry cross-check

We match the record against the institution's entry in the centralized registry.

04

Integrity check

A SHA-256 fingerprint confirms nothing on the certificate was altered.

05

Built to catch what a phone call can't.

Manual verification is slow and easy to fake. VERITAS makes forgery detection systematic.

Centralized registry cross-checks

Every accredited institution's degree records live in one auditable registry, so a certificate is checked against the source — not a scanned PDF anyone could edit.

QR-code scanning

Point a camera at any VERITAS-issued certificate for an instant, no-login authenticity check.

Tamper-evident hashing

A cryptographic fingerprint is generated for every record. Change one character and the fingerprint breaks.
